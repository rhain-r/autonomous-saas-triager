Autonomous Level 1 support engineer

It reads the logs, finds the bug, and refuses to guess.

Warrant triages your support queue against real service logs and your real source tree. Every escalation arrives with the log line and the line of code that prove it — and anything it cannot prove goes to a human instead of being quietly closed.

“Bugs closed & lost” is the number to watch. A bug sent to a human costs time. A bug closed with a help article costs the bug — nobody looks again. One wrong escalation survives every configuration, and the cited file is right only half the time; both are shown here rather than rounded away.
