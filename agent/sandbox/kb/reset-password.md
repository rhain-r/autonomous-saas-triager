# Reset your Meridian password

If you have forgotten your password, you can reset it yourself without
contacting support.

1. Go to **app.meridian.io/login** and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your Meridian account.
3. Open the email titled *Reset your Meridian password* and click the link.
4. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

## The reset link is valid for one hour

Reset links expire **60 minutes** after they are issued and can be used once.
If your link has expired, request a new one from the same screen.

## You did not receive the email

- Check your spam or quarantine folder.
- Confirm the address matches the one on your account exactly.
- Corporate mail filters sometimes hold messages from `no-reply@meridian.io`.
  Ask your IT team to allow that sender.

## Single sign-on accounts

If your organisation uses SSO, Meridian does not hold your password at all.
Reset it with your identity provider instead — see [SSO setup](sso-setup.md).
