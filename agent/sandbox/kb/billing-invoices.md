# Invoices, receipts, and duplicate charges

Invoices are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices** and are also
emailed to your billing contact when a payment settles.

## I see two charges for the same period

Check the invoice list first. Two *authorisations* with one *capture* is normal
and the pending authorisation drops off within five business days.

Two settled charges with the same amount and the same date are **not** normal.
Send the two charge references to support and do not wait for them to clear.

## Updating your payment method

Settings → Billing → Payment method. The change applies to the next renewal;
it does not retry a failed charge automatically.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5–10 business days
to appear, depending on your bank.
